## Purpose

[Product name] helps parents or legal guardians supervise a child device. It must only be used where the parent or guardian has lawful authority and has given any notices or obtained any consent that law requires.

## Information the app may process

The exact information depends on settings chosen by the parent:

- Parent account email and authentication data managed by Supabase.
- Family and device display names, pairing state, and security-session information.
- Managed-app settings, app-approval requests, block rules, scheduled limits, and safety activity events.
- SOS events created by the child device.
- Visible child-location check-ins, safe-place rules, and timestamps—only when location sharing is enabled on the child device.

The app is not designed to collect message content, call recordings, photos, microphone recordings, or hidden location data.

## Why the information is used

We use this data to authenticate the parent, pair devices, send the selected safety controls to the child device, show the parent dashboard, present SOS/safety alerts, and operate location and safe-place features when enabled.
